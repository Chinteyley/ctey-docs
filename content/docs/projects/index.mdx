I have worked on several projects across web, mobile, machine learning, and a few smaller Mac apps. Click a card if you want the longer write-up.

## Project Categories

**Web Applications** -- Full-stack sites, mostly Next.js or Angular. Job boards, notes, attendance, and study tools.

**Mobile Apps** -- Cross-platform apps in React Native and Expo. Pocket No is the refusal app. SnapPosts turns a photo into a shareable poster.

**Machine Learning** -- Smaller experiments: generative faces, wildlife photos, that kind of thing.

**macOS Apps** -- Native Mac utilities in Swift and SwiftUI. Neko is the free menu-bar kitten.
