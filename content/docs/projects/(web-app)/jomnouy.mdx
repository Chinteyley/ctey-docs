## Overview

Jomnouy (ជំនួយ, "help" in Khmer) is a study app for Cambodia's National Baccalaureate. It is in Khmer and English. Students can ask Gemini for help, snap a problem for OCR, and unlock extras through PayWay.

### What it does

- Khmer and English throughout
- Gemini chat, essay feedback, and snap-and-solve
- Practice exams, quizzes, flashcards, and older papers
- PayWay for tokens and paid features

### Tech Stack

- Next.js and TypeScript
- Supabase
- Gemini
- PayWay

### How to Use

1. Sign up and pick Khmer or English.
2. Ask the chatbot, drop an essay, or snap a problem.
3. Practice with quizzes and keep an eye on your streak.
