## Overview

Neko is a free macOS menu bar app. A tiny pixel kitten follows your mouse, stays on top of other windows, and never shows up in the Dock.

### Features

- Follows your cursor across the screen
- Stays on top, including fullscreen apps
- Menu bar only — no Dock icon
- Size and speed settings that stick between launches

### Tech Stack

- Swift
- SwiftUI
- Xcode

### How It Works

A small animation loop walks the kitten toward the cursor and picks a pose from a pixel sprite sheet.

### What I Learned

Building Neko was a good excuse to learn menu bar apps and always-on-top windows on macOS. Most SwiftUI tutorials skip that side of AppKit.
