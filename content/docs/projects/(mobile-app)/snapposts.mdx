## Overview

SnapPosts is a short path from a photo to something you can post. Capture or pick a picture, choose a theme and a caption tone, and Gemini builds the poster.

### How It Works

Start with the camera or your gallery. Pick a vibe and a caption tone. Gemini reads the photo and makes a poster you can share or save.

### Key Features

- Capture or pick a photo
- Theme and caption controls
- Gemini-generated posters
- One-tap share

### Tech Stack

- Expo
- React Native
- TypeScript
- Gemini

### How to Use

1. Open the app.
2. Take a photo or pick one from your gallery.
3. Choose a theme and caption tone.
4. Share or save the poster.
