## Overview
SnapPosts is a mobile application that turns a photo into a polished 1:1 social poster. You snap a picture or pick one from the gallery, choose a theme and caption tone, and Gemini 2.5 Flash generates a share-ready poster. The landing page states that no account is required and that a 7-day App Store trial covers the Pro features.

### How It Works

The flow is camera or gallery first, then style, then generation. After you pick a photo, you choose one of ten curated themes and a caption tone. Gemini 2.5 Flash reads the photo's composition, lighting, and subject, then produces a 1:1 poster sized for Instagram, X, and Threads. You can export or share it from the app. There is no account sign-in, activity feed, or community notifications on the current landing page.

### Key Features
- Instant photo capture or gallery pick
- Gemini 2.5 Flash poster generation
- Ten themes: Forest, Minimal, Retro, Film, Neon, Botanical, Sports, Editorial, Pastel, and Urban
- Caption tones (punchy, poetic, or minimal) plus AI caption generation
- 1:1 share-ready export for Instagram, X, and Threads
- 7-day free trial on the App Store, then Pro pricing listed on the landing page
- No account required

### Tech Stack
- Expo
- React Native
- TypeScript
- Google Gen AI SDK (Gemini 2.5 Flash)

### How to Use
1. Open the app. No sign-in is required.
2. Capture a new photo or select one from your gallery.
3. Pick a theme and caption tone.
4. Wait for Gemini 2.5 Flash to generate the 1:1 poster.
5. Export or share it from the app.

### Design Philosophy

SnapPosts was designed with the principle that creative tools should feel effortless. The interface prioritizes a minimal number of taps between opening the app and sharing a finished poster. Rather than overwhelming users with complex editing controls, the app leans on Gemini for the heavy lifting while keeping theme and tone in the user's hands.
