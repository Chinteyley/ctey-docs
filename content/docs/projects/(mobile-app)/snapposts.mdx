## Overview
SnapPosts turns a photo into a shareable poster. Snap or pick a picture, choose a vibe, and Gemini generates the rest.

### How It Works

Start with the camera or your gallery. Pick a theme and a caption tone. Gemini reads the photo and builds a poster you can share or save.

### Key Features
- Photo capture or gallery pick
- AI-generated posters and captions
- A handful of themes and tones
- One-tap sharing

### Tech Stack
- Expo
- React Native
- TypeScript
- Google Gen AI SDK

### How to Use
1. Open the app.
2. Take a photo or pick one from your gallery.
3. Choose a theme and caption tone.
4. Share or save the poster.

### Design Philosophy

SnapPosts was designed so creative tools feel effortless. The goal is a short path from opening the app to sharing a finished poster. Gemini does the heavy lifting. You stay in control of the theme and tone.
